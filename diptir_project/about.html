Who we are

Sharing Bangladesh with the world

Explore Bangladesh celebrates the country’s rivers, villages, freedom struggle, architecture, and artistic traditions. Our aim is to present Bangladesh with dignity, context, and warmth.

We invite students, travelers, teachers, and curious readers to discover a country shaped by resilience, creativity, and deep community ties.

What we focus on

  • Natural beauty and rural life
  • Historical heritage and 1971
  • Culture, language, and identity
  • Regional diversity across all 8 divisions
